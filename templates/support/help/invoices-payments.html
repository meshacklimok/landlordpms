Each lease is billed once a month (or each quarter or year) with its rent and charges.

  • Invoices are issued a few days before the month and sent to the tenant by SMS. The due date is the lease's due day; after the grace days it is overdue.
  • Payments go against the oldest invoice first. Payments from M-Pesa are recorded for you; record cash, bank or cheque payments on Payments.
  • If your role cannot confirm payments, the payment waits for someone who can.
  • Every confirmed payment gets a numbered receipt and the tenant gets a link to it.
  • Nothing is deleted. A wrong invoice is voided; a wrong payment is reversed with a reason.
  • Arrears and the call list show who owes and who to call today.
