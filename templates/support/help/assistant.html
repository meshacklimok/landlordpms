Ask answers questions about your own properties in plain words, for example "Who owes more than 30 days at Acacia Court?", "How much rent did we collect in March?", "Which units are vacant?" or "Which repairs are late?". It also answers questions about using the app, such as "How do I record a cash payment?", from these help pages, with a link to the page to use. You can ask in English or Swahili.

  • It answers only from what your role can see, on the properties you can see. If your role cannot see invoices, it cannot tell you who owes.
  • It never changes anything by itself. Ask it to record a payment, message a tenant or assign a repair and it shows a card with exactly what would be done. Nothing happens until you press Confirm; press Dismiss if anything is wrong and ask again.
  • Confirming does the same as the page would, with your role's permissions. A payment you cannot confirm still waits for review. A message's text can be edited on the card first. A card can be used once and expires after 30 minutes.
  • Under each answer, "Pages used" links to the leases, units and repairs it looked at. Check important figures there before acting on them.
  • A follow-up question remembers the last few questions. "New conversation" starts again.
  • Each person can ask 30 questions a day. Your recent questions are listed on the page and kept for 90 days.

Teaching it your own rules

An owner can write down how your organization does things under What the assistant knows in the Ask settings: office hours, how deposits are refunded, house rules, when rubbish is collected, who to see about what. Each entry is a question or topic and its answer, for all properties or one.

  • The assistant uses an entry from the next question. Change or remove it on the same page; every change is in the activity log.
  • Staff get entries on the properties they can see. Tick Tenants can be told this for entries tenants may get when they ask in the portal or on WhatsApp; leave it off for notes for staff only.
  • Phone numbers, emails and long numbers cannot be saved in an entry, because the assistant never passes them on. Say where to find them instead.
  • Up to 100 entries. Entries are sent to OpenAI with the questions they answer.

Switching it on

It is off until an owner switches it on, at the bottom of the Ask page. To answer, the question and the names and figures used to answer it (tenants, properties, units, amounts, dates and repair titles) are sent to OpenAI, which runs the GPT model. Phone numbers, emails, ID numbers, notes, documents and photos are never sent. An owner can switch it off at any time.

Answering tenants

An owner can switch on "Answer tenants" in the same settings. It is separate from the staff assistant. Tenants then get an Ask page in the tenant portal, and, where WhatsApp is set up, answers when they message your WhatsApp number from the phone on their tenant record.

  • Tenants get answers about their own homes only: rent and due day, what they owe, how to pay, their last payments with receipt numbers, and their repairs. WhatsApp messages from numbers that match no tenant get no reply.
  • A tenant on an active lease can also ask it to report a repair, or to send an M-Pesa prompt to their own phone where M-Pesa prompts are set up. Nothing is done until the tenant confirms: in the portal they press Confirm on a card (it lapses after 30 minutes); on WhatsApp they reply YES (or NDIYO) within 10 minutes, and any other message cancels it.
  • Repairs reported this way show "Assistant" as the source, and count with USSD repairs (5 a day). M-Pesa prompts count with USSD prompts (5 a phone a day, one every 2 minutes) and show "from the assistant".
  • If the tenant has set a USSD PIN, balances and payments are not given on WhatsApp; repairs and prompts still work.
  • Each phone, and each portal user, can ask 10 questions a day. STOP and START on WhatsApp still work as before and are not answered.
  • Tenants' questions are kept for 90 days and are not shown to staff.
