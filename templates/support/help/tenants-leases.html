A tenant is a person or company. A lease puts a tenant in a unit with a rent, a due day and a deposit.

  • Start a lease from the unit or tenant page. Add co-tenants and other people who pay (for example an employer).
  • Record the deposit when it is received. It is kept apart from rent and is only used for rent when you deduct it.
  • Change rent, add charges such as service or garbage, renew, transfer or end a lease from the lease page. Invoices already issued follow the change.
  • Record a move-in or move-out condition report with photos, and print a tenancy letter.
  • Print the agreement as a PDF from the lease page. It holds only what is recorded on the lease, so have it reviewed before signing, then upload the signed copy under Documents.
  • Once notice is given or the lease has ended, the move-out statement shows the balance owed, each deposit deduction, the move-out inspection, and what is due to or from the tenant. Save a copy to the lease's documents and share it with the tenant if you wish.
  • To sign without paper, choose Send the tenancy agreement for e-signature (or the move-out statement) under Paperwork on the lease page. Each tenant gets an SMS link, enters a code sent to their phone, reads the PDF, types their name and signs. You sign for the landlord on the request page. When everyone has signed, the copy with a signing record page is filed under Documents. If a tenant has stopped SMS, the page shows a link for you to share yourself. Links work for 14 days. A renewal is a new draft lease, so send its agreement the same way. Signing does not activate a draft lease; do that as usual. Only leases of two years or less with no option to renew (or month to month) can be signed this way; longer leases are signed on paper before a witness. The request page offers an evidence pack with a certificate to sign if you ever need the copy in court. Keep signed copies for six years after the lease ends.
  • The day after a lease ends, once nothing is owed either way, no deposit is held and the last month is billed, it is archived: it leaves the lease list but stays on the unit and tenant pages. Find it under "Show archived leases". If money moves on it again (for example a payment is reversed), it comes back by itself. To record something late, open it and choose Restore; it then stays in the lists for 30 days.
  • Invite a tenant to the tenant portal to see their own balance, statement and receipts.
  • Tenants on a feature phone can dial the USSD code{% if ussd_code %} {{ ussd_code }}{% endif %} to check their balance, how to pay and their last payment. It works from the tenant's main phone number only, for leases that are issued and not archived, with no PIN: keep the main phone number up to date. The menu is in the tenant's message language. When the property has an M-Pesa Paybill with a passkey, the menu also offers Pay with M-Pesa: the tenant enters an amount, confirms, and gets an M-Pesa prompt on the same phone after the menu closes. A phone gets at most one prompt every 2 minutes and 5 a day from the menu; these show on the M-Pesa prompts page as from USSD. On an active lease the menu also offers Report a repair: the tenant picks what is wrong, adds a few words and says whether it is an emergency, and gets the repair number. The request shows on the Maintenance page as the tenant's, from USSD, and the tenant gets the usual SMS updates. At most 5 reports a day per lease. A tenant who wants the menu private can choose 0. Set a PIN: from then on the menu asks for the 4-digit PIN first. Five wrong PINs lock it for an hour. If the tenant forgets the PIN, use Remove PIN under Tenant portal on their page; this removes it for that phone number with every landlord, and the tenant can set a new one.
