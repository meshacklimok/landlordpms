A tenant is a person or company. A lease puts a tenant in a unit with a rent, a due day and a deposit.

  • Start a lease from the unit or tenant page. Add co-tenants and other people who pay (for example an employer).
  • Record the deposit when it is received. It is kept apart from rent and is only used for rent when you deduct it.
  • Change rent, add charges such as service or garbage, renew, transfer or end a lease from the lease page. Invoices already issued follow the change.
  • Record a move-in or move-out condition report with photos, and print a tenancy letter.
  • Invite a tenant to the tenant portal to see their own balance, statement and receipts.
