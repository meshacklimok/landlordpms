Keep signed leases, ID copies, title deeds, insurance, permits and house rules with the property, unit, lease or tenant they belong to. Each of those pages has a Documents card with an Upload button.

  • Upload a PDF, a photo, or a Word or Excel file (.docx, .xlsx) up to 10 MB. Photos are resized and their location data removed.
  • Files are private: only your staff who may see that property can open them. Tenant documents such as ID copies need the right to see ID details, and every opening is recorded.
  • Give insurance and permits an expiry date. The Documents page and your home page list those expired or expiring within 30 days.
  • Share a lease document with its tenants, or a property document such as house rules with every tenant on an active lease there. They see it on their lease page in the tenant portal. Stop sharing at any time.
  • Replaced a file? Archive the old one. Archived documents can be restored.
