New workspaces get a 30-day trial of the Business plan.

  • Plans differ in how many units and team members you can have. Free is for up to 5 units.
  • The owner chooses a plan on the subscription page. An invoice is issued; pay it by M-Pesa to our Paybill with the invoice number as the account.
  • We record the payment and send a receipt. The period starts on the day you pay.
  • A renewal invoice comes 7 days before the period ends. Unpaid 14 days after the end, the workspace becomes read only: you can still see and export everything, but not add anything new, until you pay.
  • Changing plan mid-period gives you credit for the days you have not used.
