Bill water or electricity by meter reading instead of a flat fee.

  1. Add a meter to a property on Meters: water or electricity, the units it serves and the rate per m³ or per kWh. A prepaid token meter is not billed. A shared meter splits equally or by weight.
  2. The first reading is a starting point and bills nothing.
  3. Each month, do a reading round on your phone: one box per meter. A photo is optional. No signal in the meter room? Open the round page before you go in. If "Save readings" finds no connection, the readings are kept on the phone and sent by themselves when you are back online (photos are not kept). The menu shows how many are waiting. If one is refused, for example because someone read that meter in the meantime, the round page lists it as "needs fixing": put it back in the form, correct it and save again.
  4. Someone who may approve checks the readings. Unusual ones are flagged (lower than last time, no use, much higher, long gap) and need a note.
  5. Approved readings are billed on the next month's invoice with the readings shown, as "Metered water" or "Metered electricity".
