Bill water by meter reading instead of a flat fee.

  1. Add a meter to a property on Water: the units it serves and the rate per m³. A shared meter splits equally or by weight.
  2. The first reading is a starting point and bills nothing.
  3. Each month, do a reading round on your phone: one box per meter. A photo is optional.
  4. Someone who may approve checks the readings. Unusual ones are flagged (lower than last time, no use, much higher, long gap) and need a note.
  5. Approved water is billed on the next month's invoice with the readings shown.
