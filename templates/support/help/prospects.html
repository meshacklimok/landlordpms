Keep everyone who asks about a unit in Prospects, from the first call to the signed lease.

  • Add a prospect from the Prospects page or from a unit page. If the same phone is already an open prospect there, you are warned first.
  • Share a unit's vacancy link: people can leave their name and number on it, and they appear as new prospects for whoever shared the link.
  • After each call, add a note, tick "I spoke to them" and set the next follow-up date. Your home page lists who to call today.
  • Book viewings and record how they went. Whoever is to show the unit is told in the app.
  • Hold a unit for up to 30 days while they decide. The unit shows as Reserved and leaves the vacancy list. Release it, or it is released when a lease on it is activated.
  • Ready to sign? Make tenant creates the tenant (or uses the one with the same phone) and opens the draft lease.
  • Not going ahead? Mark them lost with a reason. The counts on the Prospects page show how many became tenants.
