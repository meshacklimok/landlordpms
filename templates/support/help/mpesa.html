Tenants pay into your own Paybill or Till. We never hold your money.

  1. Create an app on Safaricom Daraja and ask Safaricom to put your shortcode live on it.
  2. On M-Pesa settings, add the shortcode, consumer key and secret (and the passkey for a Paybill), then register the payment links.
  3. Tell tenants to use their unit's payment reference as the account number.

A payment with the right reference is recorded and receipted at once. Others wait in the M-Pesa inbox with a suggestion, for you to match or ignore. Each morning you get a summary of the day before.

With a Paybill and passkey you can also send a payment request to a tenant's phone, and tenants get a private pay link in their rent messages.
