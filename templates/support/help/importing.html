Already have your tenants in a book or a spreadsheet? Bring them in on Import instead of typing them again.

  1. Pick what you are bringing in. Tenants moving in is the quickest: each line is a tenant, the unit they live in, the rent and the day they moved in, and it creates the tenant and their lease together.
  2. Download the Excel sheet (your vacant units are already filled in), or print the form and fill it in by hand. You can also use your own spreadsheet if its columns have the same names, or a CSV file.
  3. Upload the Excel or CSV file, or take a photo of the filled form. We read the handwriting for you.
  4. Check the preview. Every row is checked and any problem is shown; nothing is saved yet. For a photo, compare each row with your paper and tick the box to confirm.
  5. Import the rows that are fine and fix the rest. You can undo an import for 24 hours.

New leases are saved as drafts for you to check and activate, unless you choose to issue them at once. For tenants who moved in long ago, give the first day of the month you start using NyumbaPMS, then add what they still owe under Opening balances.

If you only need units or tenants without leases, use those lists. Import units first, then tenants (or moving in), then opening balances.

Rather have us do it? Send us your spreadsheet in any shape and we will import it for you.
