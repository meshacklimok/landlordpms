Tenants get invoices, reminders and receipts by SMS, or WhatsApp if they agreed to it.

  • Choose which messages go out and when reminders are sent on message settings. Change the wording on templates.
  • Messages outside quiet hours (21:00 to 07:00 by default) wait until morning.
  • Send an announcement to all tenants or a group, with a preview and cost first.
  • Tenants can reply STOP to stop messages. Required messages still go.
  • SMS credit. Each SMS part is paid from your SMS credit; top up on the subscription page. Login codes and invitations are free.
