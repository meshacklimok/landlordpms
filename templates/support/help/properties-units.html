A property is a building or plot you let. It can have buildings (blocks) and units (houses, flats, shops or rooms).

  • Each unit has its rent, a type and a payment reference. Tenants use that reference as the M-Pesa account number so the payment is matched to them.
  • A property can have an owner when you manage it for someone. Owners get a monthly statement and you can record what you pay them.
  • Management fee. Set a percentage of rent collected, a fixed amount a month, or both, on the property. The fixed amount is charged every month you manage the property, even when no rent comes in. Management fees under Reports shows what you earned over 12 months.
  • Owner portal. Open the owner under Owners and invite them. They get an SMS (and an email if you have their address), log in with their phone, and see their statements, what you paid them and their properties. Tenant names are not shown to them.
  • Joint owners. On the property page, open Owners and shares and list each owner with their share; the shares must add up to 100%. Each owner's statement shows the whole property and their share of what is due.
  • Archive a property or unit you no longer let. Its history stays.
  • Your plan sets how many units you can have; see Subscription.
