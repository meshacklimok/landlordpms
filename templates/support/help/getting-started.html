A few steps get you from an empty workspace to your first rent invoice. The checklist on your home page shows which are done.

  1. Add a property on Properties: its name, where it is and, if you manage it for someone, its owner.
  2. Add units with their rent. Many units at once? Use Import.
  3. Add tenants on Tenants. A phone number is enough to start.
  4. Start a lease from the unit page: tenant, start date, rent, due day and deposit.
  5. Set up how tenants pay: connect your M-Pesa Paybill or Till, or add a payment instruction.
  6. Issue invoices: the daily job bills each lease a few days before the month starts, or press Generate on Invoices.

Then invite your caretaker or accountant from Staff so they can help.
