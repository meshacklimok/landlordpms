A few steps get you from an empty workspace to your first rent invoice. The checklist on your home page shows which are done.

  1. Add a property on Properties: its name, where it is and, if you manage it for someone, its owner.
  2. Add units with their rent. Many units at once? Use Import.
  3. Add tenants on Tenants. A phone number is enough to start.
  4. Start a lease from the unit page: tenant, start date, rent, due day and deposit.
  5. Set up how tenants pay: connect your M-Pesa Paybill or Till, or add a payment instruction.
  6. Issue invoices: the daily job bills each lease a few days before the month starts, or press Generate on Invoices.

Then invite your caretaker or accountant from Staff so they can help.

Put it on your phone's home screen

NyumbaPMS installs like an app, with its own icon, and opens without the browser bar.

  • Android (Chrome) and computers (Chrome, Edge): choose Install the app at the top of the page, or Install in the browser's menu.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

It still needs a connection: nothing about your tenants or money is kept on the phone, so a lost or shared phone holds none of it. Without a connection you see a "You are offline" page. The exceptions are a meter round or a repair report typed without a connection: it is kept on the phone until it is sent (see Water and electricity, and Maintenance). Payments are never kept on the phone; record them once you are back online. Tenants can install it the same way.

Alerts on your phone

Staff can get a short alert on their phone when something needs them: a payment to review, an M-Pesa payment that was not matched, a repair reported or given to them, a new enquiry. Open Notifications (the bell), then Turn on under "Alerts on this phone", and allow notifications when the browser asks. On an iPhone, install the app first (Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

  • An alert only says what kind of thing happened, never names, units or amounts, because others can see a lock screen. Tap it to open your notifications.
  • During your organization's quiet hours alerts wait in the bell and are not sent to the phone.
  • Logging out turns alerts off on that phone. Turn off everywhere turns them off on all your devices, for example after losing a phone.
